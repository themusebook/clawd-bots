## Short description

Your Clawd Bot companion for Solana Mobile

## Long description

ClawdMobile pairs with Clawd Bot running on your computer. Scan the pairing QR code once, then:

- follow what your bots are doing in real time;
- answer approval requests from a notification or the app;
- connect a Solana wallet through Mobile Wallet Adapter / Seeker Connect.

The app does not hold your private keys; wallet actions are signed in your wallet app. The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code.
